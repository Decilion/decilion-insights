# Decilion Insights — verify our numbers

The calculations behind the headline findings of **[Decilion Insights](https://decilion.com/insights/)** reports.

[Decilion](https://decilion.com) is a global-health consultancy working across the 33 countries of Latin America and the Caribbean, in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. Our reports are independent analytical work, and rather than ask you to take our headline numbers on trust, we publish the calculation behind each one here, so you can re-run it and check it for yourself. Transparency is our quality assurance.

The full datasets and analytical models behind each report are part of Decilion's consulting work, that is what we build for clients.
